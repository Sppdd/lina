# Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon — Short Role Summary

This file is a compact reference for AI/assistant context. It captures the key roles, responsibilities, and rules without repeating the full legal text.

## 1) Core parties

- Sponsor: Nebius B.V. (Netherlands)
- Administrator: Devpost, Inc. (New York)
- Entrants: eligible individuals, teams, and organizations
- Representative: one authorized person for a team or organization
- Judges: sponsor-selected evaluators
- Promotion Entities: Sponsor + Administrator + others involved in organizing/running the Hackathon

## 2) Eligibility

Allowed:
- Individuals age of majority in their residence country
- Teams of eligible individuals
- Organizations properly formed/incorporated at time of entry

Not allowed:
- Residents or organizations domiciled in embargoed / prohibited countries or territories (including examples like Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and other OFAC-sanctioned regions)
- Promotion Entities and their employees, agents, family, and household members
- Judges, their employers, and affiliates of blocked organizations
- Anyone whose participation creates a conflict of interest

Rule:
- Team/org entries must have one authorized Representative
- One person may be on multiple teams or also enter individually

## 3) Entry and project requirements

Entrants register on the Devpost Hackathon page and submit a Project during the submission window.

Project must:
- Run on Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud
- Use at least one NVIDIA open-source model
- Fit one of four tracks:
  - Coding and Agentic Engineering
  - Best Apps and Agents
  - Personal AI
  - Physical AI

General project rules:
- Must be functional and installable on the intended platform
- Must work as described in the video/text
- New projects are allowed; old projects must be materially updated during the submission window
- Third-party APIs/SDKs/data are allowed only if properly licensed/authorized

## 4) Submission requirements

Each submission must include:
- Link to a live demo / hosted app / test build (not required for Physical AI if hardware-access rules apply)
- Description of the app/agent
- Public repo on GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket
- Open-source license visible in the repo
- README with setup/run instructions
- Video under 3 minutes, public on YouTube
- Track selection
- Feedback on Nebius/NVIDIA tools used
- For pre-existing projects: explanation of significant updates made during the Hackathon

Additional rules:
- Must be original and owned by entrant(s)
- No restricted or unlicensed third-party content
- English required, or include English translations
- Multiple submissions are allowed only if each is unique/substantially different
- Submissions must remain available for testing through the judging period

## 5) Judging

Judging happens in two stages:
- Stage 1: pass/fail viability check
  - reasonable fit to track and use of required APIs/models
- Stage 2: scoring against four equally weighted criteria
  - Technological Implementation
  - Design
  - Potential Impact
  - Quality of the Idea

Tie-breaks:
- Highest score on first applicable criterion, then next criteria, then judge vote if needed

## 6) Prizes

Overall awards:
- Grand Prize: $20,000
- 2nd Place: $10,000
- 3rd Place: $6,000

Track awards:
- Coding and Agentic Engineering: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano
- Best Apps and Agents: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano
- Personal AI: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano
- Physical AI: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano

Bonus awards:
- Best Use of Tavily: $3,000
- City Winner Awards: $500 each (IRL city events)
- Most Valuable Feedback: $100 + NVIDIA swag pack

Important:
- Each project may qualify for one overall award or one track award plus one bonus award
- Prize awards are subject to identity/eligibility verification
- Winners must complete required forms and may owe taxes/fees

## 7) Legal / IP / conduct

Entrants agree to:
- Follow the official rules and judge decisions
- Ensure submission is original and does not infringe third-party IP or privacy rights
- Use only authorized third-party code/data
- Give the Sponsor / Devpost a non-exclusive license to judge and promote the submission
- Allow personal information and submission materials to be used in publicity

Restrictions:
- No financial/preferential support from Sponsor or Administrator for the project
- No tampering, cheating, or undermining the Hackathon
- Sponsor/Admin may cancel, suspend, or modify the Hackathon if needed
- Sponsor/Admin may disqualify entrants for violations or conflicts

- This is a Nebius + NVIDIA AI hackathon run by Devpost, open to eligible individuals, teams, and organizations.

## 10) Who does what?

- Sponsor: provides challenge, platform/resources, prize structure, and final authority
- Administrator: manages Devpost registration, submissions, and award administration
- Entrants: build and submit projects
- Representative: signs/enters on behalf of a team or org
- Judges: evaluate based on the stated criteria
- Released Parties: Sponsor, Devpost, partners, and organizers protected from many participant claims and liability exposures under the rules
