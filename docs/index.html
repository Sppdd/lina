Most AI writing tools interrupt you.
Lina waits until you ask.

Lina is a free, open-source writing helper for macOS. Type /lina and what you want, in any field of any app, and press Return. Look something up, get a question that makes you think, rewrite a paragraph or draw a picture. Nothing goes into your text until you press ⌘1.

Real use cases

Six moments where Lina earns her place. Try the line under each one in any app.

Everyone

Catch typos as you go

Finish a sentence and pause. The right word appears right above each mistake. ⌘1 fixes one and ⌘A fixes them all. If nothing's wrong, Lina stays quiet.

Thank you for you're help, I realy apreciate it.
Students, fact-checkers

Ask the web mid-sentence

Type your question where the fact belongs. ⌘1 swaps the question for an answer worded to fit your sentence, and ⌘2 adds the source.

The 2022 final ended /lina who won and how ↩
Journaling, hard decisions

A question that makes you think

/lina on its own reads what you wrote and asks one short question about it: a gap, an unstated reason, the feeling underneath. No praise, no rewriting.

…I think I should take the offer. /lina ↩
Work email

Rewrite it in a tone

Three versions of the paragraph above, in the tone you name. Pick one with ⌘1–⌘3, or /lina edit make it shorter.

send me the report now /lina rewrite polite ↩
Slides, stories

Make a picture where you are

FLUX on Nebius Token Factory draws it, and ⌘1 puts it where the command was.

/lina image a reading nook at dusk ↩
Tinkerers

Switch models without leaving the page

Every writing and image model on Token Factory, or on your local Ollama server. Switch with ⌘1–⌘9.

/lina model nemotron ↩
She remembers, listens and reads your research

Ask out loud. Write from what you gathered.

Hold ⌃⌥ to talk

Say "who won the 2022 World Cup" or "rewrite this warmer" and let go. Lina types it in as a /lina command and runs it. Speech is recognized on your Mac.

Memory

Highlight anything and press ⌃⌥ M, or type /lina remember my sister is Noor. Lina uses it every time she answers. It's stored on your Mac, and you can review it in Settings.

Use what you gathered

If you turn it on, Lina keeps the quotes you copy and reads your screenshots on your Mac. Then /lina gathered writes the next lines from your real research.

Wikipedia, with links

No Gemini key? Turn on Wikipedia and searches are answered from Wikipedia pages in your question's language, with the links. Only the question is sent.

A question when you pause

Off by default. Switch it on, and after a longer pause Lina asks her one question without being asked.

/lina bug

Something odd? Type /lina bug and a few words right where it happened. Lina writes a report of what she saw, never your text.

Meet Lina

A character, not a chatbot. Her hair tells you what she's doing.

Live web answers come from Google Gemini with Search grounding, shown with Google's search suggestions, or from Wikipedia. Commands can also run on Ollama or LM Studio on your Mac, and Lina downloads a model for you.

Privacy

She reads only when you ask. Your words stay yours.

Only when asked

Lina reads when you type /lina, hold ⌃⌥, or finish a sentence. She never reads password fields or secure input, and you can pause her in any app.

Nothing goes in by itself

Every answer waits in a card until you press ⌘1. Then it takes the command's place, and ⌘Z undoes it.

On your Mac

Memory, saved answers and gathered clippings stay on this Mac. No analytics. Commands can run on a local model.

Try it in five minutes

Download, open, type /lina.

Download

Lina.zip, unzip it, and drag Lina.app into Applications.

Seeing “Lina Not Opened”?

Lina isn't notarized by Apple yet. Click Done (not Move to Trash), then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. The one-line install below skips this.

Allow Accessibility

The welcome screen asks for it, so Lina can read the line you're on when you ask.

Sign in

Enter your email and the code Lina sends you. No keys needed. Or paste your own Nebius Token Factory key, or pick On this Mac.

# Easiest: install with one line in Terminal. No "could not verify" warning.
curl -fsSL https://sppdd.github.io/lina/install.sh | zsh
# (It's short: read it first.)

# Downloaded the zip and macOS blocks it? Clear the download flag:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lina.app

# Or build it from source (Xcode 16 command line tools):
git clone https://github.com/Sppdd/lina.git && cd lina
scripts/build-app.sh --open

Then type /lina help in any app to see every command.
